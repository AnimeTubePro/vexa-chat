Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2025 · AnimeTubePro Studio × VEXA
🔵 Data safe 🟢 No data selling 🟡 13+ only
✅ Ye official VEXA app ki Privacy Policy hai. Sawal ho to app ke Support page se contact karo.

1. Hum Kya Data Collect Karte Hain

VEXA account banate waqt hum aapse email, date of birth, display name aur username lete hain. Optional profile photo bhi add kar sakte ho. Har account ko ek internal UID milta hai jo account recovery aur support me kaam aata hai. Password kabhi bhi plain text me store nahi hota — Firebase Auth usse securely hash karta hai.

App use karte waqt ye cheezein bhi save hoti hain: aapke messages, posts, comments, likes, follow connections, call history, notifications aur presence (online/last-seen status). Ye sab aapke account ka hissa hai aur Privacy Center se control hota hai.

Profile pe aap optionally social media accounts (Instagram, YouTube, Telegram, Discord) aur game UIDs (Free Fire, BGMI) add kar sakte ho — ye publicly visible hote hain, sirf wo hi jo aap khud bharoge.

2. Location — Koi Exact GPS Nahi

Local Scan feature ke liye hum approximate ~2km area ka coarse location use karte hain. Exact GPS location, ghar ka address ya live tracking kabhi nahi hota. Distance sirf 4 levels me dikhti hai (Bilkul paas / ~500m / ~1km / ~2km) — exact meters ya direction tool ke roop me nahi.

3. Photos, Videos aur Third-Party Services

Aapke photos 3 alag Supabase Storage projects me save hote hain: profile photos (vexa-media), post images (vexa-posts) aur group photos/media (vexa-chat-guild). Sirf JPEG/PNG/WebP, max 200KB per image allowed hai.

Video messages ke liye aap apna Google Drive connect kar sakte ho — wo video aapke apne Drive me save hota hai, VEXA ke server pe nahi. VEXA sirf video ka link/metadata save karta hai. Aap Drive disconnect karke ye access revoke kar sakte ho.

4. Security aur Recovery

Recovery codes hashed form me store hote hain — koi bhi (staff bhi nahi) unhe padh sakta hai. Two-step verification (2FA) enable kar sakte ho, aur Settings → Security se dusre devices ko remote logout kar sakte ho. Recovery approval jaise sensitive steps sirf trusted server-side process se hote hain.

🔐 Aapke recovery secrets (recoveryPrivate — email, DOB, codes, password vault) tab tak safe rakhe jate hain jab tak aap khud apna account delete nahi karte. Koi auto-delete ya purge nahi hota.

5. Staff Kya Dekh Sakta Hai

Reports aur recovery requests sirf Owner/Admin/Moderator dekh sakte hain, wahi bhi moderation ke liye. Aapke private messages staff ko tab tak nahi dikhte jab tak aap khud report nahi karte. Account credentials (password/recovery codes) staff approval ke baad bhi plaintext me store nahi rehte.

6. Aapke Rights

Aap Privacy Center se apni profile visibility, message permission, last-seen control kar sakte ho. Kisi ko block/restrict kar sakte ho. Delete Account se aapka Firebase data aur Supabase photos remove ho jaate hain — tabhi recovery data bhi delete hota hai. Email support ya in-app ticket ke through data se related sawaal pooch sakte ho.

7. Bachche aur Data Safety

VEXA sirf 13 saal ya usse bade users ke liye hai. Agar kisi ko 13 se kam umar ka account milta hai, to wo account ban kiya ja sakta hai. Report karna bilkul confidential hota hai — report karne wale ka naam target user ko nahi bataya jaata.

8. Policy Updates

Ye policy time-to-time update ho sakti hai. Bade changes pe app me notice dikhaaya jaayega. Sawal ho to Support page se ticket banao ya email karo.
